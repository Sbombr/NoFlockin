# NoFlockin: A Digitial Analysis of Flock Records
The purpose of this repo is to perform a Analysis of Police Department search audits found on the haveibeenflocked.com website.
## Objective
Our project approach will investigate the PD search data recorded by the Flock automated license plate reader in correspondence with Tennessee cities. We plan to collect, clean, and organize Flock search records to find Flock and PD specific statistics for each Tennessee area. We plan to analyze things such as most common search reasons, frequency of search reasons at specific times, vocabulary used in search reasons, and more. Once the individual cities have been analyzed, we will perform a universal analysis and compare statistics across the cities.
## Data
Our current study will overlook the following Tennessee police departments: Knoxville PD, Franklin PD, Murfreesboro PD, Memphis PD, and the UTKPD. While UTKPD is not strictly a city PD, we throught it would be interesting to compare regardless.
